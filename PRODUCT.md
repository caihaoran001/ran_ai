# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal operations staff and administrators use this system during daily service operations. They manage users, 28-day service cases, check-ins, questionnaires, document reviews, dashboard signals, and diagnostic workflows, often under time pressure and sometimes from mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

Ran AI 1.0 is an internal community-operations admin system for coordinating real service cases, questionnaire intake, AI-assisted profiles and documents, human review, QiWe message workflows, check-in records, and operational monitoring. Success means staff can understand case status quickly, act safely, and inspect evidence without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, operationally precise. The interface should feel quiet, durable, and trustworthy rather than expressive or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized hero sections, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, playful illustrations, inflated spacing, nested cards, and mobile layouts that depend on desktop table widths.

## Design Principles

- Density with hierarchy: keep desktop views information-rich while making priority, state, and next action obvious.
- Evidence stays close: dashboard and review surfaces should show the case and triggering content instead of generic navigation shortcuts.
- Mobile is a first-class operations path: pages that may be opened on phones must wrap, stack, and remain tappable without horizontal overflow.
- Channel and business boundaries stay intact: UI polish must not change API calls, data contracts, or questionnaire shell isolation.
- Calm state language: color and motion should communicate status and focus, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, readable Chinese text on small screens, tap targets around 44px where practical, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable at 375px width.
